## One structural note

The two builders split the pose on purpose. One took the mid-climb moment, and
one took the moment over the crest. The reference is itself a mid-climb picture.
So variation A is the reference-faithful half, and variation B had to invent a
moment the reference never shows. Row 5 measures fidelity to the reference, so
part of variation A's advantage on that row follows from which half its builder
took.

Variation B also carries a smaller bird by design. That makes every shape smaller
in absolute units, and it makes row 7 harder to win.
